We are the “data controller” for the personal data described on this page.

What we collect and how

  • Facebook & Instagram lead forms. If you fill in one of our ads’ instant forms, Meta passes us your full name, phone number, property area or urbanisation, the type of work you need and your preferred timing.
  • Website form, WhatsApp, email and phone. When you contact us, we receive your name, contact details and whatever job details you choose to send. Our website quote form is delivered to our inbox by a form service called FormSubmit.
  • Photos of your property. If you send us photos to help us quote, we keep them with your enquiry.
  • Website cookies and similar technology. See “Cookies and website tools” below.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry, give you a quote, arrange a site visit and carry out the work. Under the GDPR, our legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and then performing that contract (Article 6(1)(b)). We may contact you by WhatsApp, phone or email about your quote and your job.

We keep customer invoices and records because Spanish tax law requires it (legal obligation, Article 6(1)(c)). Optional website cookies and the use of photos of your home in our marketing rely on your consent (Article 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time.

Who we share it with

We never sell your data or share it with anyone else for their own marketing. We only use trusted service providers who help us run the business:

  • Meta (Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp), for lead forms and messages
  • Our email provider, [EMAIL PROVIDER, e.g. Google Workspace]
  • FormSubmit, which delivers website form messages to our inbox
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts this website
  • Google, for Google Tag Manager, Google Fonts and advertising measurement

Some of these companies are based outside the European Economic Area, mainly in the USA. Where that happens, your data is protected by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

Cookies and website tools

  • Google Tag Manager. Our site uses Google Tag Manager to load Google tags, such as Google Ads conversion measurement, which help us see whether our ads lead to enquiries. These tags may set cookies in your browser.
  • Google Fonts. Our fonts load from Google’s servers, which means your browser shares your IP address with Google when the page loads.
  • Language preference. If you switch between English and Spanish, your browser remembers your choice. This is stored only on your device and contains no personal information.

You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don’t become a job: deleted within 12 months.
  • Customer records and invoices: kept for as long as Spanish tax and accounting law requires, usually up to 6 years.

Photos of our work

We love showing off finished jobs, but we only use photos of your home in our website, social media or adverts with your permission. We never show house numbers or addresses, and we’ll remove a photo if you ask us to.

Your rights

You have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, correct it, have it deleted, restrict or object to how we use it, and receive a copy to take elsewhere (data portability). To use any of these rights, just email info@marpaintingdecorating.com. We’ll reply within one month.

If you’re unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Spanish data protection authority, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD), at www.aepd.es.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your data, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
